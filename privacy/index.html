Privacy

Sopa de letras 3D · Krazel Games, Krazel Studio · Updated 3 October 2026

Settings, progress, statistics, custom puzzles and hints stay on your device. They are not sent to Krazel. Delete them by clearing game storage or deleting the app and its data.

On iPhone/iPad, Google Mobile Ads and its consent service may collect IP addresses, approximate location, device identifiers, ad/app interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement, security and service operation. Ads are requested as non-personalized. Google’s policy explains its safeguards, retention and deletion options.

Where available, review or withdraw ad consent in game Settings. Rewarded hints are optional.

Web hosting may receive IP addresses and browser information. On itch.io, itch.io’s policy also applies.

Sharing sends a puzzle’s title and words through your chosen service. Support messages are handled by GitHub; reports are public. GitHub’s policy covers its retention and privacy rights.

Privacy or deletion requests: Contact Krazel Games. Do not post personal information.
