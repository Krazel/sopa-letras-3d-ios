Privacy

Krazel Studio · Updated 4 October 2026

Settings, progress, statistics, custom puzzles and hints stay on your device and are not sent to Krazel. Clear game storage or delete the app and its data to remove them.

On iPhone/iPad, Google Mobile Ads and its consent service may collect IP addresses, approximate location, device identifiers, ad/app interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement, security and service operation. Ads are requested as non-personalized. Google’s policy explains its safeguards, retention and deletion options. Where available, manage ad consent in game Settings.

Hosting may receive IP addresses and browser information. itch.io’s policy applies on itch.io. Sharing sends a puzzle’s title and words through your chosen service. Support reports are public and handled under GitHub’s policy.

Privacy or deletion requests: Contact Krazel Games. Do not post personal information.
